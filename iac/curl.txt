==========
Rate Limit
==========
curl -i --request GET \
     --url 'https://api.render.com/v1/services?limit=1' \
     --header "Authorization: Bearer $RENDER_API_TOKEN"
Example:
> HTTP/1.1 200 OK
> Content-Type: application/json; charset=utf-8
> Ratelimit-Limit: 100
> Ratelimit-Remaining: 99
> Ratelimit-Reset: 45

Explanation:
Allowed requests:
> Ratelimit-Limit: 100
Available requests:
> Ratelimit-Remaining: 99
Seconds until request limit reset:
> Ratelimit-Reset: 45


==========
List PostgresSQL instances
==========
curl --request GET \
     --url 'https://api.render.com/v1/postgres?includeReplicas=true&limit=20' \
     --header 'accept: application/json' \
     --header "Authorization: Bearer $RENDER_API_TOKEN"


==========
Create PostgreSQL
==========
curl --request POST \
     --url https://api.render.com/v1/postgres \
     --header 'accept: application/json' \
     --header 'content-type: application/json' \
     --header "Authorization: Bearer $RENDER_API_TOKEN" \
     --data '{
  "name": "render-deploy",
  "ownerId": "$OWNER_ID",
  "databaseName": "render_deploy",
  "databaseUser": "render_user",
  "enableHighAvailability": false,
  "plan": "free",
  "version": "16"
}'
